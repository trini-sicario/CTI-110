History

Shammel Williams was rasied in Brooklyn New York his family if from Trinidad and Tobago. Joining the Army 2 years after high school , deployed to both Iraq during Enduring Freedom campaign and in Haiti during the humanitarian crisis when they were hit by an earthquake in 2010.

  1. Early Settlement and Colonial Era
  2. Lenape Roots: The area was originally home to the Lenape people, whose footpaths later became modern streets.
  3. Dutch Founding: Dutch settlers arrived in the 1620s and 1630s, naming a local town Breuckelen after a village in the Netherlands.English Control: The English took control of the area in the 1660s, renaming it Kings County.
  4. Revolutionary War: The Battle of Long Island took place here in 1776, where George Washington famously retreated across the East River.
  5. 19th Century Growth: Brooklyn experienced significant growth in the 19th century, becoming a major urban center before its consolidation into New York City in 1898.
  6. Early 20th Century: Small numbers of Caribbean immigrants arrived in New York City, laying early groundwork for a diaspora community.
  7. The 1960s–1990s: Following the Immigration and Nationality Act of 1965, large waves of West Indian immigrants from Jamaica, Trinidad and Tobago, Haiti, Barbados, and Grenada moved into Brooklyn neighborhoods as previous Jewish and Italian residents relocated to the suburbs.
  8. Little Caribbean & Little Haiti: Areas in Flatbush and East Flatbush became officially recognized cultural districts filled with family-owned Caribbean markets, bakeries, and restaurants serving jerk chicken, roti, and patties.
