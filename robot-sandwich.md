
# The Robot Sandwich

## overview
Make a peanut butter and jelly sandwich

## Materials Needed

- 2 slices of bread
- Peanut butter
- Greape Jelly
- 1 butter knife
- 1 spoon
- 1 clean plate
- 2 pieces of Paper towel 

## Instructions

1. Put the clean plate on a flat, clean table.
2. Put the bread package, peanut butter, greape jelly, butter knife, and spoon next to the plate.
3. untwist the twist-tie on the bread bag.
4. Open bread bag, remove exactly 2 slices of bread.
5. Close the bread bag retwist the twist-tie on the bread bag.
6. Put breab bag back down gently onto the table.
7. Place both slices of bread gently flat on the plate, next to each other.
8. Twist open the peanut butter container by turning its lid counterclockwise.
9. Put the peanut butter lid on the counter with the inside facing up.
10. Hold the spoon by its handle.
11. Put the spoon into the peanut butter.
12. Scoop about 1 spoon full of peanut butter.
13. Scrape the peanut butter gently on to the bottom right conor of the bread.
14. Spread the peanut butter gently and evenly on the bread using the back side of the spoon.
15. Move the spoon back and forth untill peanut butter covers most of the bread.
16. Keep peanut butter inside the edges of the bread.
17. Place the slice of bread wiht the peanut butter facing up gently on the plate.
18. Put the peanut butter lid back on the container.
19. Turn the lid with medium force, clockwise until the container is closed.
20. Pick up one piece of paper towel and clean the spoon.
21. Gently place spoon on the table next to the peanut butter container
22. pick up the greape jelly container, open the container by turning its lid counterclockwise.
23. Put the greape jelly lid on the table with the inside facing up.
24. Pick up the knife by the handle.
25. Put the blade of the knife into the jelly.
26. Scoop about 1 tablespoon of jelly into the knife.
27. Move the knife above the second slice of bread.
28. Put the jelly onto the top side of the second slice of bread.
29. Use spread jelly across most of the bread.
30. Keep jelly inside the edges of the bread.
31. Place the slide of bread jelly side up gently on the plate next to the peanut butter slice.
32. pick up the last piece of paper towel and clean off the knife, place knife gently on te table next to the greape jelly contianer
33. Put the jelly lid back on the container.
34. Turn the lid using medium force clockwise until the container is closed.
35. Gently pick up the peanut butter slice of bread.
36. Turn the peanut butter-covered bread slice upside down so the peanut butter-covered bread directly on top of the greape jelly-covered bread.
38. Match the edges of both bread slices.
39. Press gently on the top slice one time.
40. Put the sandwich in the center of the plate.
41. Throw away the used paper towels.
42. Return the bread, peanut butter and greape jelly back to their orginal areas.
43. Sound an alert to notify that the food is ready, and stand by in rest mode awating new set of orders.
